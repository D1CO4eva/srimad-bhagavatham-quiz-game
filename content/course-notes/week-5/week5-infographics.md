Srimad Bhagavatam Certification Course — Session 5: Puranas, Srimad Bhagavatam, and Canto 1

"Others may have a holy book; ours is a huge library!" — H.H. Maharanyam Sri Sri Muralidhara Swamiji

Three ways scriptures speak to us:
- Rāja Samhita: scriptures that command us like a king, they do not plead with us.
- Suhṛt Samhita: scriptures that explain spiritual truths with kindness, like a friend.
- Kāntā Samhita: divinely inspired sacred compositions, sweet like a wife's charming words.

Srimad Bhagavatam:
- Unique: it has 10 LakshanAs (characteristics) rather than the usual 5.
- Ashraya (Refuge) is its main subject.
- Each Canto covers a dominant Lakshana.
- With 12 Cantos and 10 LakshanAs, the first two Cantos are the Preface and Introduction.
- Each canto has many chapters which deal with a distinct subject, story, or portion of the story, in line with the canto's characteristic subject (lakshaṇā).

About Canto 1 — The Introduction to Srimad Bhagavatam: 19 Chapters, 809 Verses. It shows the greatness of the expounders and listeners of Srimad Bhagavatam: Narada Maharishi, Sri Veda Vyasa Bhagavan, Sri Shuka Muni, Sri Parikshith Maharaj + Ancestors, Sri Ugrashravas Suta, and the Shaunaka Rishis.
- 2 Main Stutis (praises): Kunti Stuti (8th Chapter, 26 Verses); Bheeshma Stuti (9th Chapter, 11 Verses).
- 2 Minor Stutis (praises): Women of Hastinapura (10th Chapter, 10 Verses); People of Dwaraka (11th Chapter, 4 Verses).
- 2 PralApAs (divine laments): Arjuna to Dharmaputra (15th Chapter, 23 Verses); Mother Earth to Dharma (16th Chapter, 11 Verses).

The 10 Lakshaṇās (characteristics that qualify Srimad Bhagavatham as a Maha Purana), each mapped to its dominant Canto:
1. Sarga — Primary Creation — Canto 3
2. Visarga — Secondary Creation — Canto 4
3. Staanam — Sustenance — Canto 5
4. Poshanam — Grace — Canto 6
5. Uthaya — Latent Impressions — Canto 7
6. Manvantara — Description of Manvantaras — Canto 8
7. Ishaanukatha — Stories of Bhagavan and Bhaktas — Canto 9
8. Nirodha — Dissolution — Canto 12
9. Mukthi — Liberation — Canto 11
10. Ashraya — Refuge — Canto 10

The 6 Questions: Suta Pauranika was invited to speak on the Puranas during the interval periods of the yagna. The Shaunaka Rishis ask him 6 questions to elicit the essence of all scriptures, and he answers each in turn:
1. What is the ultimate good for the people in this Kali Yuga? — Answer: Hari Bakthi (Devotion to Lord Hari).
2. Why did Bhagavan descend as the son of Devaki and Vasudeva? — Answer: Bhagavan descended to nourish devotion.
3. Please describe the Gambheera leela of Bhagavan (His leela of Creation, sustenance, and dissolution of the universe). — Answer: the Gambheera Leela is discussed in Canto 2 and 3.
4. Describe all his incarnations. — Answer: various Avatars are described across all the Cantos/Chapters (e.g. Varaha in Canto 3, Matsya and Kurma in Canto 8, Narasimha in Canto 7, etc.).
5. Describe the leelas Bhagavan did along with Balarama. — Answer: the sports of Bhagavan with Balarama are described in Canto 10.
6. Where did Dharma take refuge after Bhagavan's leela samvarana (departure)? — Answer: in this Age of Kali, Dharma has sought refuge in the name of the Lord.
