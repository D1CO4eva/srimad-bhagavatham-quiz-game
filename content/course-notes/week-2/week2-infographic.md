Srimad Bhagavatam Certification Course — Session 2: The Greatness of Srimad Bhagavatam

"Prastāna Traya" — The 3 Pillars of Sanatana Dharma's Philosophy, shown as a temple: the roof is Sanatana Dharma, and it rests on three pillars — Upanishads, Bhagavad Gita, and Brahma Sutras.

1) Upanishads: the primary authority on highest truth, and comes from the Vedas. There are 108 mainstream Upanishads — 10 of them are considered the most important.
2) Srimad Bhagavad Gita: the divine discussion between Bhagavan Sri Krishna and Arjuna on the Kurukshetra battlefield. Part of the Mahabharata (Itihasa), and has 18 chapters and 700 slokas.
3) Brahma Sutras: a synthesis of all Upanishads by Sage Veda Vyasa to show that they all carry the same message. In the form of aphorisms (sutrās are short, concise rules).

"Srimad Bhagavatam is the essence of all three pillars!" How do we know this?
1) Upanishads (Vedas): Srimad Bhagavatam has a detailed commentary of the Gāyatri Mantra and contains the crux of all Upanishads.
2) Srimad Bhagavad Gita (Essence of Mahabharata): Srimad Bhagavatam is graced by the author of the Mahabharata himself, Sage Veda Vyasa, and is the essence of the Bhagavad Gita.
3) Brahma Sutras: Srimad Bhagavatam is the commentary of the Brahma Sutras, written by Sage Veda Vyasa himself — e.g. the first shloka of Srimad Bhagavatam expounds on the second Brahma Sutra.

Other specialities of Srimad Bhagavatam:
- It is the harmonized form (sāma roopa) of the Puranas.
- "Purānam Brahma Sammitam" (SB 1.3.40): Srimad Bhagavatam is equivalent to the Vedas.
- "Nigama kalpa tarōr galitam phalam" (SB 1.1.3): It is the fruit of all Vedas in a form accessible to all.

Srimad Bhagavatam is Bhagavan Himself:
- The Padma Purana describes the greatness of Srimad Bhagavatam in six chapters (the Srimad Bhagavata Mahatmyam).
  - The 3rd chapter of that Mahatmyam details a conversation between Sri Krishna and Uddhava.
- As Krishna is about to return to Vaikunta, Uddhava asks Him not to go, in order to protect His devotees from Kali Yuga.
- Krishna says that He shall remain on Earth in the form of Srimad Bhagavatam.
