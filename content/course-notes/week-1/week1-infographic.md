Srimad Bhagavatam Certification Course — Session 1: Sanatana Dharma + Shastras + Puranas

"Others may have a holy book; ours is a huge library!" — H.H. Maharanyam Sri Sri Muralidhara Swamiji

'Sanātana' means "always (sadā)" or "that which exists (bhava)"; eternal, ever-present.
'Dharma' means "an essential quality" or "true innate nature" of an entity.

'Sanātana Dharma' is:
- Eternal, Universal, Cosmic Law — the natural order — one which can never be destroyed.
- An eternal "way of life" — codes of both internal and external conduct, ethics, and responsibilities.
- The path to realizing lasting happiness through aligning thoughts, speech, and action with Universal Values, in line with scriptures.

Quote: "vEdaishcha sarvai: ahameva vedya:" — Srimad Bhagavad Gita 15:15 — "I (Bhagavan) am known through all the Vedas."

Theme: inquiry > blind dogmas.

The Great Vedas: Any good path (shown by the Vedas), and followed with faith and loyalty, is supported for any devatā, to realize the true goal. The Vedas have 4 portions, likened to a tree:
1. Saṁhitā — Mantras (the trunk)
2. Brāhmaṇa — Rituals (the branch)
3. Āraṇyaka — Philosophy (the unripe fruit)
4. Upanishads — Knowledge (the fruit)

Our Grand Shastras — the Pramanas ("Truth as it is"), shown as a pyramid with Purānās as the widest base, Itihāsās in the middle, and Vedas at the top:
- Vedas (4 branches): means "knowledge"; divine knowledge, root of creation.
  - Rig — Hymn in Praise
  - Yajur — Worship with Sacrifice
  - Sāma — Peace to Minds (music)
  - Atharva — Mantras to ward off Evil
- Itihāsās (2):
  - Srimad Rāmāyanam — by Sri Valmiki Maharshi
  - Srimad Mahābhāratam — by Sri Veda Vyasa Maharishi
- Purānās (18): grouped by the Gunas (Sātvika, Rājasa, and Tāmasa); each has a primary Deity/Focus.

The Puranas:
- 18 Puranas total, with 400,000 total verses combined.
- Grouped by Gunas; each Purana has a primary Deity/Focus.
- All 18 were written by Veda Vyasa, except the Vishnu Puranam.
- Sātvika group: Vishnu Puranā, Srimad Bhāgavata Puranā, Nārada Puranā.
- Rājasa group: Brahma Puranā, Brahmānda Puranā, Brahma-vaivarta Puranā.
- Tāmasa group: Matsya Puranā, Kurma Puranā, Linga Puranā.
